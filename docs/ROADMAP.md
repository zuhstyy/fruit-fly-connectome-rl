# Roadmap

These are my planned experiments, with no promised completion dates. I am
working toward a frozen-policy Prologue-through-Summit clear.

| Priority | Work | Evidence to collect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Verify native Windows execution and practice restores | Repeatable room starts, held-input behavior, restore mismatches, and logs |
| 2 | Measure throughput on the target PC | Display-paced versus fast runs; 1/2/4/8-worker results; hardware and settings |
| 3 | Evaluate the saved policy on consistent starts | Checkpoint hash, seed, attempts, successes, assistance settings, and recording |
| 4 | Address observed navigation and puzzle failures | Reproducible failing room and matched before/after evaluations |
| 5 | Compare connectome wiring with alternative graphs | Matched model size, experience budget, seeds, rewards, and success rates |
| 6 | Run a complete campaign evaluation | Frozen weights, assistance disabled, native chapter events, and full recording |

Use [VALIDATION.md](VALIDATION.md) for completed checks and
[PERFORMANCE.md](PERFORMANCE.md) for benchmark procedures. Improved training
reward or changed weights alone does not establish better gameplay.
